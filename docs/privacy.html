Privacy Policy

O'zbekcha

Reyeltor-uz helps people find and list property in Tashkent. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you use the app, we may collect and store:

  • Phone number — to create your account and let buyers reach you
  • Telegram details — when you register, our Telegram bot (@Reyeltor_uz_bot) receives the phone number, first and last name you choose to share, plus your Telegram user ID and username. We use them to confirm your phone number, fill in your profile and for password recovery. We do not read your Telegram messages or contacts.
  • Email address — for your account, sign-in, and service messages
  • Listing details — property information you choose to publish (price, location, description, and similar fields)
  • Photos — images you upload for your listings
  • Location data — coordinates you place on the map when posting a listing (not continuous background tracking)
  • Saved searches and preferences — filters you save, your most recent map search (to alert you about new matching listings; you can switch this off in Favorites → Searches), the purposes you pick during onboarding, language and notification settings
  • Favorites — listings you save and their price at that moment, so we can tell you when a saved listing gets at least 3% cheaper
  • Device and usage data — a random app-generated device ID, push-notification tokens, and listing activity counts (views, saves, call and message taps) used for seller statistics and to prevent abuse
  • Error reports — technical error details (error message, app version, phone platform, your user ID; never your listing text or messages) saved in our own database so we can fix problems, or sent to Sentry if we enable it
  • Map and address services — to draw the map and find addresses, the app requests map images and searches from MapTiler, CARTO and OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, Overpass). They receive the map area you view, the address you type or the point you tap, and your IP address — not your name or phone
  • Sample photos — demo listings show photos loaded from Pexels; loading them shares your IP address with Pexels
  • Invite codes — when you open the app, it may check your clipboard once for a Reyeltor invite code so referral rewards can be credited; other clipboard content is not stored or sent

What other people can see

Published listings, their photos and location, and your public seller profile (name, photo, bio and listing statistics) are visible to anyone, including on shared web links. Your phone number and email are shown only if you keep them visible in your profile settings.

How we use your information

We use your data to operate Reyeltor-uz — for example, to show listings on the map, manage your account and coin balance, authenticate you, and improve the service.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing. We share data only with service providers needed to run the app (such as hosting, authentication, push notifications and crash reporting), under appropriate safeguards.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account and listing data while your account is active. Archived listings are permanently deleted 30 days after they are archived, together with their photos.

Get a copy of your data: Profile → Export my data creates a file with your profile, listings, saved homes, saved searches and coin history, and lets you save or send it.

How to delete your account

You can delete your account and all associated data directly in the app:

  1. Open the Reyeltor-uz app and go to your Profile
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the profile screen
  3. Tap the red "Delete All Data" button
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Warning: This action will permanently delete all your data, including:

  • Your account profile and personal information
  • All your listings and posted properties
  • Photos associated with your listings
  • Your saved favorites
  • Your coin balance and transaction history
  • Your check-in records
  • Your saved searches, notification settings and push tokens

This action cannot be undone. If you need additional help or have questions about account deletion, please contact us at eeshbaev@outlook.com.

Children

Reyeltor-uz is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact us
